# Brand kit delivery notes (historical snapshot, 2026-11-09)

> **Historical snapshot.** This is the review note that came with the brand kit on
> 2026-11-09, before the owner approved it on 2026-11-10 and before the app adopted it. It
> is not maintained. Current rules, files and colours: [docs/reference/brand.md](../../../docs/reference/brand.md)
> and [brand/tokens.json](../../tokens.json).

## Artwork status

The original is an owner-supplied Flow raster concept (`source/originals/sevjala.jpg`,
2048 × 2048, sha256 `2cf60b92cdf32ae9d67e554668149d9da5faa2c54312b72536bed03e1296f9d3`):
a navy database, a fully enclosing fishing net and a USB fishing rod. The SVGs are
hand-authored reconstructions and derived designs, not original Flow vectors or
pixel-identical copies. No automatic raster tracing is used; editable geometry is in
`source/geometry.py`.

The reconstruction regularizes curves, mirrors the net, spaces the tiers, connects the USB
handle to the rod and omits tiny fill fragments. Net crossings use local SVG masks to create
transparent gaps; no white background is baked into the vector marks. If a downstream editor
has limited mask support, use the PNG export or flatten the masks in that editor.

Full, compact and micro marks were delivered at different detail levels. Navy #14233B is a
normalized colour close to the reference, not an exact sampled colour. Blue #2457C5 was the
dashboard's light-theme accent; the dark-theme accent was #7AA2FF. "A safety net for your
data" was illustrative guide copy, not a historical tagline.

## The app at the time

The app used an older blue "database wrapped in links" icon (decision D-088): `winres/icon.png`
(256 px, the exe icon), `tray/brand.png` (32 px; the tray drew the status colour as a dot in
its bottom-right corner, ringed in white) and `ui/public/icon.png` (128 px; the tab icon and
the dashboard header). The kit noted that installing a new static PNG alone would not
recreate the tray's status overlay, and that the overlay and its white ring must be kept.

Dashboard colours then: light background #F6F7F9, card #FFFFFF, text #1C2330, muted #5B6474;
status green #1A7F37, yellow #A15C00, red #C62828, grey #6B7280. Tray dots were separate:
green #2EA043, yellow #E3A008, red #D03030, grey #8A8A8A. Typography was the system UI stack.

## Proposals the owner reviewed

Mark geometry, small-size recognition, the Plus Jakarta Sans Semibold wordmark, clear space
(a quarter of the visible mark height) and starting widths (full 128 px, compact 64 px,
horizontal lockup 400 px, micro below). The kit was not a trademark clearance.
